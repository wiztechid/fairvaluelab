# Corpus Intelligence — CekValuasi

Track corpus health by useful coverage, not URL count.

Measure:
- eligible universe;
- valued vs review/indicative/reference/unvalued/error;
- freshness distribution;
- provenance completeness;
- sector coverage;
- ticker pages with substantive evidence;
- stale/duplicate/thin pages;
- internal-link reachability;
- search demand and reader task completion.

Scaling is allowed only when quality distribution remains controlled.
