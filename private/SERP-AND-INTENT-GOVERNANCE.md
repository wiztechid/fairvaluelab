# SERP and Intent Governance — CekValuasi

Primary ticker intent: understand whether current valuation appears cheap/fair/expensive under transparent model evidence, not receive a personalized trade instruction.

Guard against cannibalization among:
- ticker valuation page;
- sector page;
- methodology explainer;
- educational valuation articles;
- comparison tools.

One dominant canonical page per primary intent. Supporting content should deepen, not duplicate, the same answer.
