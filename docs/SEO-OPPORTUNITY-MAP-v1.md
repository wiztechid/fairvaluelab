# CekValuasi SEO Opportunity Map v1

Status: public-safe planning registry. This file records intent ownership and publication order, not proprietary scoring formulas.

## Freshness contract
- Freshness Checked: 2026-09-28
- Lookback Window: rolling 12 months (2025-09-29 through 2026-09-28)
- Recheck Before Draft: REQUIRED for every page, even when this registry previously marked the opportunity as valid.
- Recheck means inspecting the current SERP/query family again before drafting for material changes in ranking publishers, content recency, intent composition, tool presence, and competing CekValuasi owners.
- SERP Churn labels are directional governance labels, not proprietary opportunity scores:
  - Low — limited meaningful movement/new competition in the 12-month evidence window.
  - Medium — meaningful updates/new entrants exist, but the intent landscape remains relatively open or fragmented.
  - High — multiple fresh/updated results, strong publishers/tools, or rapid recent content movement; old opportunity assumptions must not be reused without fresh evidence.

Freshness expires for drafting purposes: a historical PASS never grants permanent publication permission.

## Acquisition thesis
CekValuasi grows through three connected search territories:
1. Valuation — understand whether market price is reasonable.
2. Syariah Discovery — narrow the DES universe into research candidates.
3. Risk — translate a chosen setup into position and portfolio-risk consequences.

Search journey: Search → Understand → Verify → Plan.
Product journey: Temukan → Cek Valuasi → Atur Risiko → Pantau.

## Intent ownership / cannibalization guard
- /harga-wajar-saham/ owns fair-value outcome/tool intent.
- /nilai-intrinsik-saham/ owns intrinsic-value concept intent; it must not become a second fair-value calculator.
- /metode-valuasi-saham/ owns method-selection intent; PER/PBV/DCF/DDM discussion supports this owner.
- /margin-of-safety-saham/ owns discount-to-fair-value interpretation.
- /saham-syariah-undervalued/ owns DES-specific undervalued discovery.
- /screener-saham-syariah/ owns screening/tool intent.
- /cara-mencari-saham-undervalued/ owns generic discovery methodology.
- /position-sizing-saham/ owns position/lot sizing.
- /manajemen-risiko-saham/ owns the broader risk framework.

Rule: one search intent has one canonical owner. Supporting pages may mention another owner's topic but must not duplicate its complete answer.

## Internal-link path
Syariah Discovery → Fair Value → Margin of Safety / Methodology → QSTP.
Educational pages must link to the most relevant product or next-decision page, not mechanically to every product.

## Scale rule
The first 10 are the controlled launch set. Expansion toward 50–100 pages requires each proposed URL to pass the Content Publication Gate independently. Keyword variation alone is not sufficient reason to create a page.

## Boundary
Exact opportunity scoring weights, proprietary Scanner gates, ranking heuristics, evidence penalties, state-transition logic, and reusable private research IP are intentionally excluded from this public repository.


## 12-month freshness notes — 2026-09-28
- Generic valuation/harga-wajar intent: High churn. Multiple fresh or recently updated Indonesian results appeared in Jul–Sep 2026, including method-heavy educational pages. CekValuasi must compete with tool + explanation + evidence/confidence, not another generic definition.
- Nilai intrinsik / method-selection intent: High churn. Fresh 2026 pages increasingly combine intrinsic value with multiple valuation approaches, increasing overlap risk with /harga-wajar-saham/ and /metode-valuasi-saham/.
- Generic undervalued discovery: High churn. Fresh Sep 2026 content directly targets how to find undervalued stocks. Treat /cara-mencari-saham-undervalued/ as supporting methodology unless a pre-draft SERP recheck shows a stronger gap.
- Syariah × valuation / undervalued: Medium churn. Current evidence includes 2026 academic and market content, but the landscape remains more fragmented than generic valuation. This remains the preferred differentiation territory, subject to recheck.
- Syariah screener: Medium churn. Active screeners already expose sharia filters, so CekValuasi must differentiate through research prioritization, explainability, and the Scanner → Fair Value workflow rather than generic filtering.
- Margin of Safety: Medium churn. Fresh 2026 explainers exist; the CekValuasi page must connect MoS to valuation uncertainty/evidence rather than repeat the formula only.
- Position sizing / risk: High churn. Fresh 2026 calculators and publisher explainers already cover the basic formula. QSTP acquisition content must emphasize IDX-aware lot sizing, chosen invalidation/SL, and portfolio-risk consequences.
- DES/reference freshness: treat OJK as primary authority. DES changes periodically and can also change incidentally; any page depending on current sharia eligibility must verify the current OJK decision at draft/publication time.

## Freshness gate invariant
No page may move from opportunity registry to drafting solely because it appears in the first-10 list. The owner must first re-run a current SERP check and record the date. If intent, competition, or product fit materially changed, the candidate returns to HOLD/MERGE/REJECT review before drafting.
