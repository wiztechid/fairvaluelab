# Site-Specific SOP — CekValuasi

## Scope
Financial valuation research for Indonesian equities, initially centered on the configured DES universe.

## Reader promise
Help readers inspect valuation evidence; do not present automated output as certainty, personalized advice, or guaranteed target price.

## Product rules
- Answer-first UX: current price, valuation range/base, status, then evidence.
- Clearly distinguish valuation, technical context, catalysts, and market actors.
- Display uncertainty and degraded states prominently.
- Keep methodology reachable from every valuation report.
- Permanent ticker pages must contain substantive ticker-specific evidence.

## SEO rules
- One canonical permanent URL per ticker when migration is activated.
- No indexable thin/error placeholder pages.
- Sitemap contains only canonical indexable pages.
- Page title/meta/schema must agree with visible data.
- Sector/hub pages must add navigational or analytical value.

## Financial trust rules
- No hidden market anchoring.
- No cherry-picking only favorable methods.
- No silent stale data.
- No claim of official target price unless actually sourced and attributed.
