# Trust Governance

CekValuasi is a research aid, not a guarantee of intrinsic value or investment outcome.

## Trust requirements
- Show source/freshness quality.
- Show uncertainty and model disagreement.
- Separate facts, derived values, and assumptions.
- Keep limitations visible.
- Do not imply official endorsement from OJK, IDX, issuers, or data vendors.
- Avoid false precision in reader-facing copy.
- Corrections affecting published valuation require an auditable record.

## Reader agency
Present evidence so readers can make their own investment decisions. Do not convert confidence scores into personalized buy/sell instructions.
