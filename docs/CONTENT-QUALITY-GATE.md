# Content Quality Gate — CekValuasi

Financial content quality includes numerical integrity, reader utility, and search-intent integrity.

## Reader-first gate
A page passes only if a reader can quickly identify:
- what question the page answers;
- the direct answer or useful method before unnecessary background;
- assumptions, limitations, and uncertainty where material;
- what evidence/data supports financial claims;
- what the sensible next step is.

Do not pad pages to a target word count. Do not manufacture FAQs, examples, or sections merely to make a page look comprehensive.

## Financial integrity gate
Where applicable:
- ticker/company identity is correct;
- visible numbers match canonical data;
- status/confidence language matches engine state;
- source/freshness context is available;
- methodology/limitations are reachable;
- no unsupported investment promise or disguised BUY/SELL signal appears;
- historical claims preserve point-in-time limitations.

## Original-value gate
The page must contribute at least one meaningful form of value: a useful tool, original/canonical data presentation, clearer decision framework, evidence synthesis, worked example, comparison, or product-integrated workflow.

Rephrasing existing pages, keyword substitution, or generic AI prose is not original value.

## Product-bridge gate
A product CTA is allowed only when it is the natural next decision. The page must remain useful even if the reader does not click. Do not distort the answer to force a conversion.

## Link gate
Internal links point to relevant canonical intent owners and products. No orphan launch pages. No boilerplate link stuffing.

Failing pages must not be promoted merely to increase page count, publishing cadence, or sitemap size.
