# Monetization Governance

Monetization must never alter valuation logic, status, confidence, rankings, or source selection.

## Rules
- Ads/affiliate elements are visually distinct from research output.
- No ad placement may imitate valuation controls or buy/sell actions.
- Sponsored relationships must be disclosed.
- Monetization scripts must not block access to methodology, limitations, or source/freshness information.
- Revenue optimization cannot justify thin page creation.
- Financial outputs remain identical whether or not a page is monetized.
